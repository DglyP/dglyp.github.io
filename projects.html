My Work

Recent Work

You can find some of my amazing projects here, if you want more information about them just check out my GitHub, Instragram or the videos included in some of them.

Using MediaPipe for a Parallax 3D Effect

Experience where I take a facemesh from a webcam feed and use it to track the movements of the user in order to give a Motion Parallax effect. Useful for Augmented reality experiences.
Test it here

3D Reconstructions using Colmap and MeshLab

An outdoor scene reconstructed from 1983 frames from a video. This mesh was obtained by feature extraction on Colmap and then parsed in MeshLab
See the full image here

Realtime 3D Visualizer

Developed using THREE.JS and GLSL. A research based web simulation that will show you the color composition and light elevation of a specific video. Available also as a VR experience, tested in Oculus Quest 2
Check out the code

HoloBrain 2.0

Project developed with the help of Microsoft Garage. A Holographic brain floats in space to teach Neuroanatomy students about its parts and how to read and understand MRI.
Cool video here

AR Car Tracking

Using Vuforia Image Trackers, an Arduino developed car and a HoloLens device, we created this OpenXR Application that lets you play a Mario-Kart-esque game where you get powerups when you hit the special orbs
Check the code here

Online Clinical Simulation

As a UBC faculty of medicine research project, we used augmented reality to give life to Annie, the mannequin that would teach students how to perform PCR and other medical techniques. We used ARCore and ARKit for mobile development.
Cool video here

Facial Emotion Recognition

Using Deep Convolutional Neural Networks and a whole lot of reading about emotions, I developed a 65% accurate Facial Emotion Recognition software using OpenCV and CUDA. It even shows nice emojis
