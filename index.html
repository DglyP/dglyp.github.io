About Me

Who Am I?

Hey I'm Degly Pava, Senior Extended Reality Specialist and CEO in Tokyo, Japan. I've mentored 700+ developers globally, created 291M+ AR interactions, and built partnerships with MIT, KDDI, and Meta.

Featured at MWC 2025 with Apple Vision Pro demo • Developed STYLY platform hosting 50,000+ XR experiences • Global XR community leader

My expertise spans cutting-edge Extended Reality experiences that have garnered attention from industry leaders across Japan, USA, and Colombia. I've worked with gaming giants like CAPCOM and Nintendo, led AR development for Universal Studios Japan, and pioneered AR glasses technology through Qualcomm's Developer Pathfinders Program.

Beyond XR, I've developed 2 Android applications and contributed to numerous open source projects, demonstrating versatility across mobile and web platforms. I also created a Unity package library for seamless Apple Vision Pro Spatial Persona integration, simplifying complex visionOS development workflows for the developer community.

From coordinating Unity-based space exploration games for Apple Vision Pro to ensuring quality standards for a €12M European XR Master's program, I bridge innovation with education.
