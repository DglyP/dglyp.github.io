About Me

Who Am I?

Hey I'm Degly Pava, an Extended Reality Evangelist and Senior Unity Engineer for Apple Vision Pro in Tokyo, Japan. I turn ideas into immersive reality through Unity, visionOS, C#/C++, AR/VR/MR product strategy, and developer ecosystem work.

Meta Horizon Start Mentor • MIT Reality Hack best XR mentor • 20K+ XR platform downloads • 291M+ AR interactions • Speaker on XR glasses and spatial computing

My expertise spans cutting-edge Extended Reality experiences that have garnered attention from industry leaders across Japan, USA, and Colombia. I've worked with gaming giants like CAPCOM and Nintendo, led AR development for Universal Studios Japan, and pioneered AR glasses technology through Qualcomm's Developer Pathfinders Program.

Beyond XR, I've developed 2 Android applications and contributed to open source projects, demonstrating versatility across mobile and web platforms. I developed multiple OpenUPM packages for emerging technologies including Apple Vision Pro and PICO XR devices, adopted by 15+ companies in Japan's XR market.

From coordinating Unity-based space exploration games for Apple Vision Pro to ensuring quality standards for a €1.58M European-funded XR Master's program, I bridge innovation with education.
